# Introduction to Multipliers

Multipliers let you increase the XP gained by members in specific roles, channels, or globally for your server.  

This is useful for rewarding certain members or encouraging activity in specific parts of your server.

---

## How Multipliers Work

- **Role Multipliers**: Assign extra XP to members with a specific role.  
- **Channel Multipliers**: Increase XP earned in selected channels.  
- **Server Multiplier**: A global boost applied to all XP earned on the server.  
- **Duration**: You can set multipliers to expire after a certain period.

---

## Examples

- Give top leaderboard members extra XP for being highly active.  
- Encourage chatting in certain channels by applying a higher XP multiplier there.  
- Apply a temporary global server boost during events or special occasions.

---

Multipliers are a flexible way to guide behavior, reward activity, and create temporary incentives for your community.
