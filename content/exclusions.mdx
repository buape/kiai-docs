# Exclusions

Exclusion settings can be found in `/settings > Denylist`.  

Exclusions apply to **all XP methods** (messages, voice, drops, etc.) and are a way to control who can earn XP in your server. Kiai supports three types of exclusions: **Channels**, **Roles**, and **Users**.

---

## Channels

Channel exclusions are useful for channels that:  
- Are often spammed or flooded  
- Are not intended for meaningful XP activity  

**Example:**  
Excluding `#memes` ensures users don’t gain XP from posting there, keeping XP focused on productive discussion channels like `#general` or `#help`.

---

## Roles

Role exclusions remove XP earning from specific groups of users.  

**Example:**  
You might exclude moderators or bots from earning XP to prevent them from dominating the leaderboard.

---

## Users

User exclusions apply on a case-by-case basis. This is helpful for:  
- Members who are abusing the XP system  
- Disruptive behavior that would unfairly affect progression  

**Example:**  
A user repeatedly spams messages to gain XP quickly. Excluding them stops further XP accumulation while maintaining fairness for others.

Exclusions can also be used strategically to prevent top-ranking leaderboard users from always dominating, encouraging more competitive and active participation across your community.
