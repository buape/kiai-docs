# Kiai Basics

Kiai turns participation into progress.  
The more active members are in your server, the more experience points (XP) they earn, and the higher they climb on the leaderboard.

---

## Core Concepts

- **Experience Points (XP)**  
  XP is earned through activity such as sending messages or participating in voice chats.

- **Levels**  
  As members accumulate XP, their level increases. Levels represent their growth and activity within the server.

- **Rewards**  
  Server admins can assign roles, perks, or other bonuses that unlock when members reach specific levels.

- **Leaderboards**  
  Leaderboards highlight your most active members and can add a sense of friendly competition.

---

## What Is a Leveling System?

If you have never used a Discord leveling bot before, a leveling system is a way to turn participation into visible progress.  
Members receive XP for being active, and their progress is tracked over time.

---

## How It Works

1. **Participate:** Send messages, talk in voice channels, and engage with your community.  
2. **Earn XP:** Kiai awards XP based on activity.  
3. **Level Up:** Reaching XP milestones increases your level.  
4. **Unlock Rewards:** Levels can grant roles, perks, or recognition.

---

## Why Leveling Matters

Leveling systems encourage engagement.  
When members know their activity contributes to progression, they are more motivated to participate and stay active.

Kiai supports multiple ways to earn XP, including messaging, voice activity, XP drops, and more. This provides more flexibility and variety than many other leveling bots.
