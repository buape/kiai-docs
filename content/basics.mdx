Kiai is about turning participation into progress. The more you contribute to your server, the more XP you earn, and the higher you climb.

## Basic Building Blocks
- **Experience Points (XP):** Every message, every voice chat, every bit of activity adds up.  
- **Levels:** As XP accumulates, you “level up” - showing your growth to the community.  
- **Rewards:** Server admins can set up role rewards or perks tied to certain levels.  
- **Leaderboards:** Friendly competition - see who’s the most active in your server.   

## What is a leveling system?

If you’ve never used a Discord leveling bot before, here’s the general idea:  
A leveling system turns participation into visible progress.

## The Flow
1. **Participate:** Send messages, hop into voice, or join events.  
2. **Earn XP:** Every action gives you points.  
3. **Level Up:** With enough XP, your level increases.  
4. **Get Rewards:** Unlock custom perks, roles, or simply bragging rights.  


## Why It Matters
Leveling systems motivate people to stick around. Instead of passively chatting, members know their activity helps them progress, which creates a stronger and more active community.
Kiai excels as a leveling bot by providing you, the server admin, with many different ways for users to participate and earn xp that other bots may not provide.
