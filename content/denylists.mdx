# Denylists

Configure denylists in `/settings > Denylist`.

Denylists apply to **every way of earning XP**, including messages, voice activity, reactions, and XP drops. Kiai supports three types of denylists: **Channels**, **Roles**, and **Users**.

Each denylist type holds up to 25 entries.

---

## Channels

A channel denylist stops XP from being earned in specific channels. You can add text channels, channel categories, voice channels, and forum channels.

Channel denylists work well for channels that:
- Get spammed or flooded often
- Are not meant for meaningful activity

**Example:**
Add `#memes` to the channel denylist so members do not earn XP there. This keeps XP focused on channels like `#general` or `#help`.

---

## Roles

A role denylist stops XP from being earned by members who hold a specific role.

**Example:**
Add your moderator role to the role denylist so moderators do not earn XP and dominate the leaderboard.

---

## Users

A user denylist stops XP from being earned by a specific member.

User denylists work well for:
- A member who is abusing the XP system
- Disruptive behavior that would unfairly affect a member's progression

**Example:**
A member spams messages to gain XP quickly. Add them to the user denylist to stop further XP gain while keeping the system fair for other members.
