# Rank Cards

Rank Card settings can be found in `/settings > Leveling Settings`.  
Here you can customize the **background**, **primary color**, and **secondary color** of rank cards.

> **Note:** Rank card customization is a **premium-only feature**. Server rank card customization requires **Server Premium**, while user rank card customization requires **User Premium**.

---

## Colors Explained

- **Primary Color**:  
Applies to the user’s **current level, current XP, current rank, progress bar, and the ring around the avatar**.

- **Secondary Color**:  
Applies to **everything else**, including XP to next level, level text, username text, the `#` in front of rank, and the outline of the level bar.

---

## Server vs. User Rank Cards

- **Server Rank Card (Server Premium)**:  
Sets the default appearance for all users in the server. Requires **Server Premium** to enable.

- **User Rank Card (User Premium)**:  
Premium users can override the server settings with their own **background, primary color, or secondary color**. Requires **User Premium**. Their custom settings take priority over the server defaults.

---

Rank Cards are a visual way to show progression and identity in your server. Custom colors and backgrounds help make them unique and engaging for your community.
