# Leaderboards

## Regular Leaderboards

Run `/leaderboard [type] [time]` to view a leaderboard for your server.

- **type**: `XP and Levels` (default), `Messages Sent`, `Voice Minutes`, or `XP Streaks`.
- **time**: The time period the leaderboard covers. Defaults to all time.

A leaderboard does not update on its own. Run the command again to see current standings.

On an XP leaderboard, a member who has reached the level cap shows `(Max)` next to their level instead of a level number past the cap.

> XP Streak leaderboards are only available for the all-time period.

> Weekly and monthly leaderboards are a gradually-rolled-out feature. If the `time` option does not offer a weekly or monthly period in your server, either the feature has not reached your server yet, or your server admin has not turned it on in `/settings > Time Leaderboards`.

## Automatically Updating Leaderboards

An auto-leaderboard is a leaderboard message that Kiai keeps up to date on its own, refreshing about once a minute.

- `/auto-leaderboard create <channel> <type> [time] [description]` — Creates an auto-leaderboard in a channel. `description` adds up to 800 characters of text above the leaderboard.
- `/auto-leaderboard delete <leaderboard>` — Deletes an existing auto-leaderboard.

Both commands require the `Manage Server` permission.

> Creating an auto-leaderboard requires **Server Premium**.

## Leaderboard Position Roles

Leaderboard position roles differ from leveling rewards. Kiai grants and removes them automatically based on a member's **live rank** on the leaderboard, not their level, and re-checks them every time a member's XP changes.

Manage leaderboard position roles with `/leaderboard-roles add`, `/leaderboard-roles remove`, `/leaderboard-roles list`, and `/leaderboard-roles sync`. These commands require the `Manage Server` permission.

- `/leaderboard-roles add <role> <position> [time]` — Assigns a role to a leaderboard position. `position` can be First, Second, Third, "First through Third," or "First through Fifth." `time` can be All, This Week, or This Month, and defaults to All.
- `/leaderboard-roles remove <reward>` — Removes a leaderboard position role.
- `/leaderboard-roles list` — Lists every leaderboard position role.
- `/leaderboard-roles sync` — Re-checks and re-applies every leaderboard position role.

Kiai's own role must sit above the role you want to assign in your server's role list, and Kiai needs the **Manage Roles** permission to run `/leaderboard-roles sync`.

Each role can be assigned to only one leaderboard position.
