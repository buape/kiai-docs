Kiai is a Discord leveling bot that brings gamification to your server.  It’s designed to reward activity, encourage participation, and make communities more fun.

## What Kiai Does
- Tracks activity like chat messages and voice time  
- Awards XP that builds toward levels  
- Rewards (roles and perks)  
- Ranks members on leaderboards  
- Provides mass configurability

Servers use Kiai for it's diverse configuration options, and it's ability to keep members engaged in various of ways. Not just through messages but with voice xp, and xp drops.
Every feature can be configured independently offering extensive customization befitting to any discord server.
