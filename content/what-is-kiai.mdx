# What Is Kiai?

**Kiai** is a Discord leveling and progression bot built to bring gamification to your community.  
It rewards activity, encourages participation, and helps servers create engaging, long-term progression systems.

---

## What Kiai Does

Kiai provides a complete set of tools for tracking activity and rewarding members:

- Tracks chat activity and voice participation  
- Awards XP that contributes toward leveling  
- Supports customizable rewards, such as roles and perks  
- Ranks members on configurable leaderboards  
- Offers extensive configuration and control over every system  

---

## Why Servers Use Kiai

Servers choose Kiai for its flexibility. Every major feature: XP, leveling, voice tracking, drops, leaderboards, and more can be configured independently.  
This allows communities of all sizes to tailor the bot to their specific needs, whether they want simple leveling or a fully customized progression system.

Kiai keeps members engaged through multiple activity types, not just messages. Voice XP, timed XP drops, and adjustable multipliers give servers a variety of ways to reward participation.
