# What Is Kiai?

Kiai is a Discord leveling and progression bot. It rewards member activity and helps your server build long-term engagement.

---

## What Kiai Does

Kiai tracks activity and rewards members for it:

- Tracks chat messages, voice participation, and reactions
- Awards XP for this activity
- Converts XP into levels
- Grants rewards, such as roles, money, and custom messages, at set levels
- Ranks members on a leaderboard
- Lets you configure each feature on its own

---

## Why Servers Use Kiai

Each major feature works on its own: XP, leveling, voice tracking, XP drops, and leaderboards. You can configure each one to fit your server, from simple leveling to a fully customized progression system.

Kiai also tracks more than messages. Voice XP, reaction XP, XP streaks, timed XP drops, and multipliers give you several ways to reward member activity.
