# Basic Commands

Once Kiai is in your server, you can start interacting with it immediately.  
Here are the **core commands** every user should know:

- **`/rank`**  
  View your current level and XP progress, including how much XP is needed to reach the next level.

- **`/leaderboard`**  
  See the top members in your server and how they rank against each other.

- **`/preferences`**  
  Open your personal Kiai settings. Here you can adjust notification preferences, control display options, and manage how Kiai interacts with you.

---

Start with `/rank` to see your progress, then use `/preferences` to tailor your experience.
